2. Appointments

Customers must provide accurate contact information and select the requested service, barber where applicable, date, and time. A booking request is not final until Fresh Fade Hub confirms availability.

Please arrive a few minutes before your appointment. Late arrival may reduce the time available for the service or require the appointment to be rescheduled.

3. Services and Pricing

Displayed prices are in South African rand and are based on the selected service. Prices may change, but the price shown in a confirmed booking request will be used as the expected price unless the requested service changes.

Final pricing may vary where additional work is requested or the condition and length of the hair requires extra products or time. We will explain any material price change before continuing.

4. Cancellations and Rescheduling

Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule. We may release appointments when a customer is significantly late or does not arrive.

Repeated no-shows or late cancellations may require a deposit or limit future online bookings. Any deposit requirements will be explained before payment is requested.

5. Payments

Payment is due at the shop unless another arrangement has been confirmed in writing. Accepted payment methods and any deposit terms will be communicated before the appointment.

6. Calendar Links

After submitting a booking request, the website may provide Google Calendar and Apple Calendar-compatible calendar files. These calendar entries are convenience tools only and do not replace confirmation from Fresh Fade Hub.

Customers remain responsible for checking the date, time, barber, service, and location in their calendar and for updating or removing the entry if the appointment changes.

7. Customer Health and Safety

Please tell your barber about relevant allergies, skin sensitivities, medical conditions, or products that may affect the service. Fresh Fade Hub may decline or modify a service where it would be unsafe or unsuitable.

8. Customer Conduct

Customers must treat staff and other customers respectfully. We may refuse service or end an appointment where there is harassment, threatening behaviour, discrimination, or conduct that creates a safety concern.
